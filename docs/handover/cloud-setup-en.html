Connecting your till to the cloud

A new version of Arkom POS lets you see your shop — sales, stock, repairs — from a phone or any browser. This is everything you need to do, once.

About 15 minutes Your sales, stock and repairs stay exactly where they are The till keeps working with no internet
  1. 1

    Take a backup first

    Nothing in this process touches your data, but take one anyway — it costs a minute and it is the difference between a bad afternoon and no afternoon at all.

    In the till, open Ajustes → Copia de seguridad and press Copiar ahora. Wait for Última copia to show today's date and time.

    Worth doing while you are there Press Elegir carpeta… and pick a USB stick or a synced folder. That gives you a second copy somewhere other than this computer — which is the copy that matters if the computer itself is the thing that fails.
  2. 2

    Install the new version

    Close the till program. Run the file we sent you — Arkom POS Setup 1.2.0.exe — and let it finish.

    Do not uninstall anything first. This installs over your current version and keeps the same shop data, in the same place. You will not be asked to set the shop up again, and nothing needs importing.

    Open the till and check the bottom of the settings screen says 1.2.0. Your articles, sales, repairs and used devices should all be exactly as you left them.

    If anything at all looks missing after this step, stop and call us before you sell anything. Do not re-run the installer.
  3. 3

    Create your account

    On a computer or a phone, open the address below and choose Crear una cuenta. You will need an email address you can open right now, a password of at least 8 characters, and the name of your shop.

    We will send you a confirmation email. Open it and follow the link — the account is not active until you do.

    This login is completely separate from the PIN you use at the counter. The PIN stays on the till and never leaves the shop.

  4. 4

    Generate the code that links your till

    Signed in, go to Tills in the menu and press Link a till. Give it a name you will recognise later — "counter", "upstairs" — and press Generate the code.

    You will get a code that looks like 2YA7-AGBG-C4ZQ. Keep that page open, or copy the code somewhere, and go to the till.

    The code is shown once. We do not store it and we cannot look it up for you. If you lose it, nothing is broken — just press the button again for a new one. It stops working after 7 days, and after the first till uses it.
  5. 5

    Enter it on the till

    In the till, open Ajustes → Nube. There are two boxes.

    In Dirección, replace whatever is there with the address below — exactly, with no slash at the end. In Código de enlace, paste the code from step 4. Then press Enlazar la caja.

    When it works the card shows ENLAZADA and a time under Último envío. Everything the shop has already sold starts going up on its own. There is nothing to wait for and nothing else to press.
  6. 6

    Check it from your phone

    Open the same address on your phone and sign in. Within a minute or two you should see today's takings, your articles, and anything in the workshop.

    Add it to your home screen and it behaves like an app. It is only ever a window onto the shop — nothing you do there can change the till.

If something does not look right

The till says it could not link
Nearly always the address. It must be typed exactly as above, starting with https:// and with nothing after .app — no slash, no spaces. If the address is right, the code may have expired: generate a new one.
The dashboard is empty, or is missing recent sales
The till sends in the background every few minutes. Press Enviar ahora on the Nube card to push immediately. If it still looks behind, check the shop's internet — the till will catch up by itself once it is back, and no sale is ever lost waiting.
The internet goes down
Keep selling. The till does not use the internet to work — it only uses it to send a copy upstairs. Everything you ring up while it is down is queued and goes up when the line returns.
The confirmation email never arrived
Check the spam folder first. If it is not there, tell us the address you used and we will look.

What this changes, and what it does not

Nothing about how you sell
The counter works exactly as it did. No new step, no waiting for a network, no change to tickets or to the printer.
What leaves the shop
Sales, stock movements, repairs and used-device records — the figures the dashboard shows. Staff PINs, device unlock codes and the photographs you take of phones and ID documents stay on the till and are never sent.
Unlinking
Desenlazar on the same card stops the till sending. It removes that till's credential and nothing else — your shop's data stays on the till either way.
